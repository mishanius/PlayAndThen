PlayAndThen

Screen time that turns into learning time

While your child watches YouTube or YouTube Kids, a short learning game appears at the pace you set. Your child plays for about a minute, and then the video continues.

For children aged 3 to 7, on Android. Start free with 3 games.

How it works

  1. 1

    Set it up once

    PlayAndThen uses Android's Accessibility Service to know that a video is playing. You turn it on after a full explanation in the app, and you can turn it off at any time.

  2. 2

    Your child watches

    Works with YouTube and YouTube Kids. Choose how often a game appears: every few minutes, or on every new video.

  3. 3

    A game, then the video

    A short learning game appears on top of the video. Your child plays for about a minute, and then the video continues.

11 learning games

Numbers, colors, words and letters. Eight play in English and three in Hebrew. A friendly monster reads each question aloud. Every right answer gets a celebration; after a miss, the game shows the right answer and moves on to a new question.

Parents stay in control

On when you want it

Switch PlayAndThen on and off from its home screen. All settings sit behind a parent lock.

Off only with a parent

While PlayAndThen is on, a notification shows a Turn off button that first asks a parent question. When it is off, one tap turns it back on.

You choose how often

A game every few minutes, at the pace you set, or on every new video.

Your data

  • No account and no ads; the app reads no advertising ID.
  • Video titles and screen content are processed on your device and are not collected.
  • From the first launch, the app sends crash reports, loads its settings and checks the subscription, each with a random ID for this install.
  • Game and settings events, purchases and your approximate region go to Firebase Analytics only after you accept the in-app disclosure.
  • With the Pro language filter on, a title's language is checked on the device and Google gets only the detected language, never the title.
  • To have your data deleted, write to mishaannatech@gmail.com.
