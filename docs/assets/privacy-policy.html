Privacy Policy for PlayAndThen

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Overview

PlayAndThen is a parental control app for Android. It helps parents manage screen time on YouTube and YouTube Kids by asking the child to complete a short educational game before or while watching videos. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short

  • We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • There is no account to create, no advertising, and the app does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • Video titles and screen content read by the Accessibility Service are processed only on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase, and checks your subscription with RevenueCat and Google Play.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

1. Information processed only on your device

PlayAndThen uses Android's Accessibility Service on YouTube and YouTube Kids to:

  • read video titles, to detect the language of a video (with Google ML Kit, which runs on the device);
  • read the playback state, to know when a video starts and ends and how long it has been watched;
  • find screen elements such as the video player, to pause or skip a video and to show a game.

This information is used only for these purposes, on your device. It is not stored by the app, not sent to any server and not shared with anyone. Google ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostics about how the language-detection library performs (for example the device model, app version and error codes), but never the text it analyses.

2. Information stored on your device

  • App settings, such as the chosen games, game frequency, allowed video languages and the child's language.
  • Game progress and statistics, such as which game stages were answered correctly, used to adapt the games and to show the statistics screen.
  • Whether you accepted the accessibility disclosure, your promotion status (kept in encrypted storage), and a cached copy of your subscription status kept by the RevenueCat library.

This data stays on the device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings > Apps > PlayAndThen > Storage > Clear storage).

Android backup: if backup to your Google account is turned on for your device, Android may include these settings and game statistics in your device backup and restore them on a new device. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

3. Information sent to service providers

The app uses the following services. They process information on our behalf and under their own privacy policies.

Google Firebase Analytics

We receive anonymous usage statistics to understand how the app is used and to improve it:

  • App events: a game was shown or completed (which game, how many rounds, the game trigger mode), a video was skipped, a search redirect happened, the accessibility service was enabled, the disclosure was accepted, and which setting was changed (its name, not its value).
  • Information the Firebase SDK collects automatically: a random app-instance identifier, device model, operating system and app version, device language, approximate region (country or city, derived from the IP address), and session and engagement information.

Firebase Analytics does not receive video titles, screen content, detected video languages or the advertising ID.

Google Firebase Crashlytics

If the app crashes, a crash report is sent: technical details of the error, device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier. We use it only to find and fix bugs.

Google Firebase Remote Config

The app downloads app settings (for example, whether a promotion is active). This uses a random Firebase installation identifier and the app version.

RevenueCat and Google Play Billing

If you buy a subscription, Google Play processes the payment. We never see your card or payment details. RevenueCat receives the purchase details from Google Play (product, purchase token, dates and status) and a random anonymous user identifier, so the app can confirm and restore your subscription.

Privacy policies of these providers: Firebase, Google, RevenueCat.

4. Children's privacy

PlayAndThen is set up and controlled by a parent, and the child plays its games on the device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the app asks for no names, accounts or contact details, shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID. The only information sent off the device is the anonymous usage statistics, crash reports and subscription checks described above, used to run, improve and fix the app. A parent can ask us to delete this information at any time (see "Contact us").

5. How long we keep information

Information on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. Information held by our service providers is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above and is deleted according to their retention settings.

6. Security

Information sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Promotion status on the device is kept in encrypted storage.

7. Your choices

  • You can turn off the PlayAndThen accessibility service at any time in the Android accessibility settings.
  • You can delete all on-device data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • You can ask us to delete the anonymous statistics linked to your device by contacting us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
